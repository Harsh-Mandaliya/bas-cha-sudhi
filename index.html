Our belief

We think the best conversations, the boldest ideas and the oldest friendships all start the same way. With a warm glass in your hand and someone saying, “bas, ek cha sudhi.”

01 — Our story

From a roadside tapri to your favourite corner.

It started in 2019 with one brass kettle, a secret masala recipe from Ba’s kitchen and a tiny stall. Today we brew over 800 glasses a day, but every pot is still simmered slow, by hand, exactly the way Ba taught us.

04 — The ritual

Four steps between a garden and your first sip.

  1. 01

    Pound

    Whole spices crushed in a stone mortar at 6 AM, every single day.

  2. 02

    Boil

    Water, leaf and ginger rolled to a boil — the colour has to be just right.

  3. 03

    Simmer

    Milk in, brought up three times. Patience is the secret ingredient.

  4. 04

    Pour

    Strained from a height for that frothy top, served steaming hot.

10 — Good to know

Questions? Ask over chai.

Do you take table reservations?

Yes! Book through the form below or WhatsApp us. Walk-ins are always welcome too — we keep half the tables free.

Is there vegan or sugar-free chai?

Every chai can be made with oat or almond milk, and sweetened with jaggery, honey or nothing at all.

Can I work from the café?

Absolutely. The loft has charging points at every seat and fast Wi-Fi. Weekday mornings are the quietest.

Do you deliver?

Yes — find us on Zomato and Swiggy. Our chai blends from the shop are delivered free above ₹499.

Can I host a private party?

The Garden Room seats up to 30 guests for birthdays, meetups and small celebrations. Drop us a message for packages.
